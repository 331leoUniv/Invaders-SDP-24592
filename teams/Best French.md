## 2. Team Requirements
We are developing the **Item System** of the Space Invaders game. This module manages the lifecycle, attributes, spawning logic, and effects of all in-game items. It acts as the backbone for player enhancements and consumable rewards during gameplay.

---

1. **Twin Cannon Upgrade (Double Barrel):** Spawns dynamically midway through Level 2. Upgrades the player's standard single-projectile fire into a dual-stream volley.
2. **Chameleon Hull (Color-Shifter):** Unlocks in Level 3. Causes the player's ship sprite to cycle through a dynamic palette of colors every 2 seconds.
3. **Cluster Grenade Launcher:** Equips the ship with an explosive ordnance sub-weapon. Launches slow-moving grenades that detonate upon impact or at a set altitude, dealing localized Area-of-Effect (AoE) splash damage.
4. **Overclocked Fire Rate (Hyperfire):** A temporary combat buff that significantly reduces the player ship's weapon cooldown/delay, allowing continuous high-frequency rapid fire to overwhelm advancing enemy waves.
5. **Orbital Annihilator (Doomsday Laser):** A high-tier ultimate item introduced in Level 7. Charges and unleashes a massive, full-screen penetrating energy beam that obliterates all standard enemy ranks and incoming enemy projectiles in its path.
